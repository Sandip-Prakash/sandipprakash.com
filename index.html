How it works

From conversation to conversion.

Step 1

Listen

Start with customers, not opinions. Interview until the pattern is obvious.

Samsung TV Plus: 5 personas and 4 competitive teardowns reframed the problem from content acquisition to attention retention.

Step 2

Position

Find the one story the market can repeat, then pressure-test it with sales and customers.

Step 3

Ship and scale

Turn the story into a system: pages, launches, enablement, experiments, and the metrics to prove it.

Millennial Labs: ROAS from 2× to 7× and a ~200% turnaround from loss to profit.
